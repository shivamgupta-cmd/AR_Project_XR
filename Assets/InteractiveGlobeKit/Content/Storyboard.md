# Interactive Globe Storyboard

| Scene | Topic | Visual / VFX | Learner action | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Our Earth | Globe fades in with a soft cyan rim and slow rotation. | Observe. | A globe is a spherical model of Earth. It helps us understand places, directions and how our planet moves. |
| 2 | Explore | Full textured globe remains visible. | Drag to rotate and pinch to zoom. | Rotate the globe to explore continents and oceans from every direction. |
| 3 | Equator and poles | Red equator, pole markers, tilted axis and moving cyan particles appear. | Select each feature. | The equator divides Earth into northern and southern hemispheres. Earth's axis is tilted by about twenty-three and a half degrees. |
| 4 | Latitude and longitude | Cyan coordinate grid, yellow Prime Meridian, tropics and polar circles illuminate. | Turn overlays on and off. | Latitude measures north or south of the equator. Longitude measures east or west of the Prime Meridian. |
| 5 | Hemispheres | Transparent northern/southern overlays appear independently. | Compare halves. | The equator separates north and south. The Prime Meridian helps separate east and west. |
| 6 | Locate places | Glowing coordinate pins and labels appear. | Tap a pin. | Every location on Earth can be described using a latitude and longitude coordinate. |
| 7 | Day and night | One side remains illuminated while the opposite side darkens; Earth rotates. | Start/stop rotation. | Day and night occur because Earth rotates. The side facing the Sun has daylight while the opposite side experiences night. |
| 8 | Review and quiz | Important overlays appear together, followed by a five-question check. | Answer the quiz. | Review the equator, poles, hemispheres, coordinates, axis tilt, locations, and the cause of day and night. |
