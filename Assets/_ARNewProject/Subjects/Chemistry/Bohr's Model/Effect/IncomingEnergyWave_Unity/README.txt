INCOMING ENERGY WAVE - UNITY

1. Copy Assets/IncomingEnergyWave into your Unity project's Assets folder.
2. Wait for Unity to compile.
3. Go to Tools > Incoming Energy Wave > Create Ready Prefab.
4. Unity creates Assets/IncomingEnergyWaveGenerated/IncomingEnergyWave.prefab
5. Drag that prefab into the scene.
6. Assign your electron Transform to Target.
7. Put the prefab outside the atom where the energy should start.
8. Press Play.

Recommended values:
Points 48
Amplitude 0.08
Frequency 5
Wave Scroll Speed 10
Travel Speed 1.8
Width 0.025

For a stronger snake motion:
Amplitude 0.12-0.18
Frequency 3-4

Use On Energy Absorbed to call your electron jump animation.
For stronger glow in URP, enable Bloom.
