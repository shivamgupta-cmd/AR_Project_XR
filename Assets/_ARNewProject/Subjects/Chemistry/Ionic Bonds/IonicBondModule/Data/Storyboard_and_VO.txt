IONIC BOND — NaCl MODULE FLOW

1. INTRO / SHOW ATOMS
Visual: Sodium atom and chlorine atom appear side by side and rotate slowly.
VO: "An ionic bond forms when one atom transfers an electron to another atom. Let us see how sodium and chlorine form sodium chloride."
Method: ShowAtoms()

2. HIGHLIGHT OUTER ELECTRON
Visual: Sodium's outer electron glows and pulses.
VO: "Sodium has one electron in its outermost shell, while chlorine needs one electron to complete its outer shell."
Method: HighlightElectron()

3. ELECTRON TRANSFER
Visual: Glowing electron travels from sodium to chlorine with a yellow-orange trail.
VO: "Sodium transfers its outer electron to chlorine."
Method: TransferElectron()

4. FORM IONS
Visual: Atom models switch to Na+ and Cl- with 3D charge symbols and a brief flash.
VO: "After losing an electron, sodium becomes a positively charged ion, Na plus. Chlorine gains the electron and becomes a negatively charged chloride ion, Cl minus."
Method: FormIons()

5. ELECTROSTATIC ATTRACTION
Visual: Na+ and Cl- move toward each other; glowing attraction line appears.
VO: "Because opposite charges attract, the sodium and chloride ions are pulled strongly toward each other by electrostatic attraction."
Method: StartAttraction()

6. CRYSTAL LATTICE
Visual: Many lattice parts build one by one into a 3D crystal structure.
VO: "Many sodium and chloride ions arrange themselves in a repeating three-dimensional crystal lattice."
Method: BuildCrystalLattice()

7. FINAL MODEL
Visual: Complete NaCl lattice rotates slowly.
VO: "This strong attraction between oppositely charged ions forms an ionic bond and produces sodium chloride, commonly known as table salt."
Method: ShowFinalLattice()

TIMELINE ORDER
ResetModule -> ShowAtoms -> HighlightElectron -> TransferElectron -> FormIons -> StartAttraction -> BuildCrystalLattice -> ShowFinalLattice
