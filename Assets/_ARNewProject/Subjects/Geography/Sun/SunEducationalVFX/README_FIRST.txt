EDUCATIONAL SUN VFX V2 - UNITY 2022 URP

This version includes the supplied Sun_01.fbx and true 3D interior layers.

SETUP
1. Wait for Unity to finish importing and compiling.
2. Open Tools > Educational Sun > Build Complete FBX + 3D Cutaway Package.
3. Drag Prefabs/EducationalSun_3D_Cutaway.prefab into your scene.
4. Enable HDR and Bloom for the video-style glow.

The physical layers are real thick geometry, not flat textures:
- Solid Core
- Radiative Zone Shell
- Convective Zone Shell
- Photosphere Shell

Use SunCutawayController to switch views and reveal the layers.
Use SunVFXController.TriggerSolarFlare() for a manual flare and CME.
