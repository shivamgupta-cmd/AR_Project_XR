--- PLANETS ---

1. Mercury (Mercury.mp3)
   "Mercury follows the innermost orbit and has the shortest year of all the planets."

2. Venus (Venus.mp3)
   "Venus follows the second orbit and takes longer to complete one revolution around the sun."

3. Earth (Earth.mp3)
   "Earth follows the third orbit and completes one revolution around the sun in about 1 year."

4. Mars (Mars.mp3)
   "Mars follows the fourth orbit and takes about 687 Earth days to complete one revolution."

5. Jupiter (Jupiter.mp3)
   "Jupiter follows the fifth orbit and has the longest orbital period of the inner five planets."

6. Saturn (Saturn.mp3)
   "Saturn follows the sixth orbit and is famous for its extensive ring system."

7. Uranus (Uranus.mp3)
   "Uranus follows the seventh orbit and takes about 84 Earth years to complete one revolution."

8. Neptune (Neptune.mp3)
   "Neptune follows the eighth and outermost planetary orbit and takes about 165 Earth years to orbit the sun."
